## 0. Method and data provenance (read first)

- Recipe data for Factorio 1.1, Factorio Space Age ("2x1" dataset), Dyson Sphere Program and Satisfactory was taken from the open-source FactorioLab project's extracted data files, `public/data/<id>/data.json` at https://github.com/factoriolab/factoriolab (1.1, 2x1, dsp, sfy, coi). These are community extractions of each game's own recipe data, not the official wiki or Lua prototypes read directly. [F] Wiki-versus-extraction agreement was not cross-checked. Local copies and the scripts (`an.py`, `mil.py`) are in `C:\Users\Meta\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\F--Projects-CultLib\84318a97-ab31-4505-a924-6bc6eb76f31c\scratchpad\eyes-ag8-recipe-graphs\`.
- Counting rules (mine, applied identically across games): technology/research and recycling recipes excluded; raw = outputs of mining-flagged recipes or items no recipe produces; "consumer products" of an item = distinct output items of recipes that take it as an input; "intermediate" = non-raw item with at least one consumer product; "single-use" = exactly one consumer product; "terminal" = non-raw with zero consumers. Buildings, equipment and vehicles are items like any other, so a hub's consumer count includes buildings, not only goods. Depth = steps from raw using the cheapest recipe (min over producing recipes of 1 + max input depth); loops (barrelling, breeding, fuel cycles) make a few depths meaningless (Factorio 1.1 "Raw fish" shows depth 9).
- Satisfactory: the file lists default and alternate recipes together (296 recipes, no alternate flag). Two figures are given; neither is the exact default-recipe set. The all-recipes figure understates single-use (alternates add consumers); the second, a subset restricted to recipes whose id equals their single output id (125 recipes), is a rough proxy for default recipes and may drop some defaults.
- Captain of Industry was fetched but not analysed: its file has no mining flags, so raw/depth were meaningless (1 raw, depth up to 23 through waste loops). X4 wares and GregTech were not tabulated (see section 6).
- Shares are of intermediates that have at least one consumer; terminal counts are in section 1.

## 3. Ammunition and weapon chains

### Factorio (1.1 and Space Age) [F, FactorioLab data; mechanics partially also from wiki.factorio.com/Military_science_pack]
- Military science pack: 1 Grenade + 1 Piercing rounds magazine + 2 Wall -> 2 packs, 10 s; raw per pack: iron plate 5.75, copper 0.5, coal 5, stone 10 [F wiki.factorio.com/Military_science_pack]. FFF-275 (0.17): the old recipe used a Gun turret; the author says turret was too expensive against the other ingredients, replaced by 2 Walls; Piercing rounds and Grenades stay because they are useful for defence; military upgrades consolidated so the Rocket silo no longer requires them [F https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-275].
- Ammo tiers (1.1 recipe inputs), all from the data: Firearm magazine <- 4 iron plate; Piercing rounds <- Firearm magazine + steel + copper; Uranium rounds <- Piercing rounds + U-238; Shotgun shells <- copper + iron; Piercing shotgun shells <- Shotgun shells + copper + steel; Cannon shell <- steel + plastic + explosives; Explosive cannon shell <- steel + plastic + explosives (2x); Uranium cannon shell <- Cannon shell + U-238; Explosive uranium cannon shell <- Explosive cannon shell + U-238; Rocket <- circuit + explosives + iron; Explosive rocket <- Rocket + explosives; Artillery shell <- 4 Explosive cannon shell + Radar + 8 explosives (Space Age: Radar + Calcite + 4 Tungsten plate + 8 explosives); Grenade <- iron + coal; Cluster grenade <- 7 Grenade + explosives + steel; Flamethrower ammo <- steel + crude oil; Atomic bomb <- 10 Rocket control unit (Space Age: Processing unit) + 10 explosives + 30 U-235 (Space Age 100).
- Space Age ammo additions: Railgun ammo <- steel + copper cable + explosives; Tesla ammo <- Supercapacitor + plastic + electrolyte; Railgun <- tungsten plate + superconductor + quantum processor + fluoroketone; Tesla gun <- holmium plate + superconductor + plastic + electrolyte; Capture bot rocket (biter capture).
- Intermediates exclusive to weapons: none. Computed "military-exclusive" non-combat intermediates (items whose every consumer is military): 0 in both 1.1 and Space Age. Explosives (sulfur + coal + water chain) has 9 consumer products in 1.1, 8 military and 1 non-military (Cliff explosives); in Space Age 10, 9 military and 1 non-military [F].
- Military-category items that are intermediates to other military items, with consumer products (1.1) [F]:
  - single consumer product: Firearm magazine -> Piercing rounds; Shotgun shells -> Piercing shotgun shells; Cannon shell -> Uranium cannon shell; Rocket -> Explosive rocket; Defender capsule -> Distractor capsule; Distractor capsule -> Destroyer capsule; Rocket launcher -> Spidertron (non-military consumer).
  - two or three: Explosive cannon shell 2 (Artillery shell, Explosive uranium cannon shell); Grenade 3 (Cluster grenade, Military science pack, Cliff explosives); Piercing rounds 3 (Defender capsule, Military science pack, Uranium rounds); Laser turret 2 (Discharge defense, Personal laser defense, both equipment).
  - Space Age adds: Explosive cannon shell 1 (Explosive uranium cannon shell); Rocket launcher -> Rocket turret; Rocket turret -> Spidertron; Tesla gun -> Tesla turret; Capture bot rocket 2 (Biolab, Captive biter spawner).
  - Count: of the 11 military-category intermediates listed for 1.1, 7 have exactly one consumer product (Firearm magazine, Shotgun shells, Cannon shell, Rocket, Defender capsule, Distractor capsule, Rocket launcher); Space Age 14 listed, 10 with one consumer product (the seven above (Rocket launcher now feeds Rocket turret), plus Explosive cannon shell, Rocket turret and Tesla gun). The military end items themselves (the other 33-40 items) are terminal.
- Hub inputs shared with civilian goods: Steel plate has 14 of 59 (1.1) / 17 of 74 (Space Age) consumer products in the military set; Coal 3 of 8 / 3 of 9; Plastic bar 2 of 4 / 4 of 7; Electronic circuit, Iron gear wheel, Iron/Copper plate feed ammo, turrets and civilian goods alike. Space-Age top-tier weapons (Railgun, Tesla gun, turrets) use Superconductor, Quantum processor, Tungsten plate, Holmium plate, which are also hub items in Space Age (Superconductor 13 consumers, Tungsten plate 13) [F].

## 5. Developer design statements and player criticism

- Factorio FFF-275 (0.17 science changes) [F https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-275]: the author keeps Engine units and Advanced circuits in the Chemical pack because "useful across many other recipes" (engines needed for pumps, trains, cars); Low density structure is in the Utility pack because it is an ingredient in several advanced personal-equipment items; Rails fit the Production pack as a late-game, high-throughput ingredient; the Gun turret was removed from the Military pack as too expensive relative to its other ingredients; Processing units in the Utility pack dropped from 3 to 2 as "just too many"; military upgrades were consolidated so the Rocket silo needs none, "a separate branch" for dealing with enemies; design principles listed: meaningful choice between tech paths, smoother difficulty, avoid forcing upgrades players won't use.
- Factorio FFF-351 [F]: contains no statement on recipe design (checked, off-topic). Other Friday Facts on recipe sharing were not found by search [S].
- Dyson Sphere Program: no developer devlog or interview on intermediate sharing or chain depth was found [S]. Producer Zhou Xun statement on combat intensity is above. Player discussion of centralised smelting (shared basic materials) versus per-product chains: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1366540/discussions/0/3183488858477589433 [S, opinions].
- Satisfactory: no Coffee Stain statement on single-use parts found; only third-party description that alternate recipes come from Hard Drives (each offers a choice of two recipes) [S: https://xgamingserver.com/blog/satisfactory-best-alternate-recipes-tier-list/]. Alternate-recipe count in the data: 296 recipes against about 125 id-matched defaults [F].
- GregTech and bloated chains: no source found that complains specifically about single-use intermediates [S]. Closest items: an FTB thread poll on GregTech (about 51% liked, 33% disliked, 236 voters) with a post complaining that GregTech's recipe changes force progression through the IC2/GregTech tree to use other mods, a gating complaint rather than a single-use one [S https://forum.feed-the-beast.com/goto/post?id=277694]; a GTNH reviewer saying each overcome inconvenience is replaced by a new one and that some resources become unneeded once automated [S, https://git.iczelia.net/journal/5-january-2024/ and moddex review]; the Bigger Industrialisation pack advertises "semi-realistic chemical chains with lots of steps, intermediates, byproducts" and simplifies chemistry before HV tier by design [S https://www.modpackindex.com/modpack/133597/bigger-industrialisation]. Factorio-mod pairs aimed at intermediate depth: Cmers' Vanilla Revamp "more use of complex intermediate products rather than large quantities of basic parts"; Baron Many Little Things; Alternative Intermediates [S, mods.factorio.com]. The GregTech canonical complaint named in the brief was not located in text.

## Bearing on the open question (facts only)

- Factorio 1.1: 35 of 84 intermediates (42%) have exactly one consumer product; 22 (26%) have five or more. Space Age: 53 of 147 (36%) and 49 (33%). DSP: 34 of 84 (40%) and 25 (30%). Satisfactory: 16% to 22% single-use depending on counting alternates.
- Weapon and ammunition intermediates in Factorio 1.1: 11 military-category intermediates, of which 7 have a single consumer product (Firearm magazine, Shotgun shells, Cannon shell, Rocket, Defender capsule, Distractor capsule, Rocket launcher); 0 non-combat intermediates are military-exclusive (Explosives has 8 of 9 consumers military and one civilian, Cliff explosives). In Space Age, 0 military-exclusive non-combat intermediates; Explosives 9 of 10 military.
- DSP combat ladders (Dark Fog): 8 of 13 combat intermediates have one consumer product (the ammo-box, shell-set, missile-set and capsule ladders), Combustible/Explosive/Crystal Explosive Unit are consumed only by combat items (3, 3, 2 consumers); the base inputs of combat recipes are general hubs (Circuit Board, Steel, Processor, Titanium Alloy, Super-magnetic Ring).
- Satisfactory: 3 of 94 intermediates in the id-matched subset are ammo-exclusive (Black Powder, Smokeless Powder, Packaged Turbofuel); Smokeless Powder has 4 ammo consumers.
- Tier ladders are mostly built by stacking the same ammo type (magazine -> piercing -> uranium; shell -> explosive -> uranium shell; Magnum -> Titanium -> Superalloy box), each stacked step being a single-consumer item; none of the three games has a weapon-only intermediate class outside those ladders and the explosives families.
- NMS: both 15.6M-unit trade goods draw about 20 products each with shared Quantum Processor, Thermic Condensate, Enriched Carbon and Nitrogen Salt, and are sold rather than used; players report an in-hour 2.5 billion unit run before nerfs and describe the two as the standard late-game earners; no developer statement was found.
- Factorio's own post on the military pack says it was built from items "useful across many other recipes" and treats military as an optional branch not required for the rocket.

# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-29)

### Features

- First test build since 2020, for Windows and Linux (x86-64), published to GitHub by hand. Flight under the weaker gravity field: thrust is a tenth of its authored push and drag applies only above top speed, so orbits coast instead of decaying. Keyboard turning ramps up and down, and thrusters join a turn in order of the yaw they give per unit of push. Weapons aim along your view's pitch and the crosshair follows; weapon groups bind to the mouse buttons and number row the first time you fly a ship. Stardust and dithered particles draw under the nebula fog. Undocking no longer flings the ship into the void. Console: `fly <hull>` puts you in a freshly fitted ship of that hull. `ModTools/AetherDb` is the mod and data tool.

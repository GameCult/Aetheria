## Aether

Aetheria’s namesake is the now-defunct scientific concept of aether, a propagation medium for light waves. In Elysium, aether is real, and this static reference frame can be manipulated to enable phenomena considered impossible in the universe as we know it. The Aetheric Field permeates all of Elysium and is essential to its function as an inescapable garden-prison for sentient life. It is an extremely high-level construct of the very technology humanity began to scratch the surface of when it started messing with FTL travel.

### What the Field Answers To

The aether does not obey rules the way flat-space physics does. It is anchored by the cognition of the minds living inside it ([[Cognitive Interfacing]]), and it settles toward whatever those minds take for granted. Reality has been demonstrably stranger ever since the Timeless’ contingency shunted us here, and some, [[Megiddo]] loudest among them, conclude that Elysium is not real at all: a simulation held together by its residents’ attention. They may well be right about the mechanism. It changes nothing. There is no going back, so this is all we have, and it is as real as it needs to be.

What we can say about how the field behaves:

- **Consensus beats will.** The aether bends toward what most nearby minds expect. A lone [[Esper]] in deep space can do things the same esper cannot do on a crowded station, where ten thousand people assume the ordinary. This is why flat space holds in the core worlds and frays at the periphery ([[Pseudospace]]).
- **Belief is load-bearing.** If the public is convinced a product works, it works. If a maker persuades enough people it has invented spatial expansion, the tamper-proof black box that makes your cargo bay bigger on the inside can be empty, or full of nonsense, and the bay is still bigger. By the same mechanism, the corporate campaign to discredit espers is not only public relations: a public that is sure espers are frauds makes them weaker.
- **It answers to a self, not to compute.** Processing power alone does nothing. The aether responds to a mind that wants something, which is why the accelerated brainwashing that makes uploads compliant also destroys their ability to touch it. You cannot simulate a will, so you have to harvest one ([[Necrotech]]). The physics does not care how the mind was obtained.
- **Small effects go unopposed.** An effect too minor for anyone to hold an opinion about meets no resistance. Nobody’s worldview depends on their fridge’s Carnot efficiency, so a sliver of borrowed will can quietly cheat thermodynamics there. The more an effect contradicts what people expect, the more mind it takes to hold it.
- **Information density distorts.** Dense concentrations of information warp the field, so data cores and Aethernet hubs are pockets of local strangeness.
- **Holding takes attention.** An effect lasts only while something keeps attending to it.

**"A rimed rubble pile is half a Bloom already."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: sessile colonial filter-feeder, reef-builder. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Rime is a crust of tiny colonial animals that grows on rock: frost-white, glittering, branching into combs that face the current. A rimed belt looks frozen. Each colony is millions of polyps sharing one mineral skeleton, the way coral does [new].

**Eats.** Glim and krill larvae, combed out of the current as it flows past.

**Moves.** It doesn't; it rides its rock.

**What a pilot sees.** Pale glittering crust on rocks and on old wrecks. Thick rime means the current here is rich and has been for a long time. Rime on a wreck means the wreck is old.

**On sensors.** A rock that is slightly warm and slightly too reflective.

**How it hurts.** Mining a rimed rock sets the colony off: it fires a spray of mineral spicules, micro-shrapnel that scours the miner's sensors and drills [new]. Rime also cements rubble together, so rimed rock breaks into bigger chunks than it should.

**Yield.** Rime lattice, a mineral-organic cement. [[Bloom]] builders like rimed rubble piles because the rime has already bound them; a rimed asteroid takes less processing to become a habitat shell [new]. Miners and Bloom contractors fight over the same rocks.

**Lives.** Asteroid belts, the inner rings around gas giants, and every wreck that has been sitting long enough. Old battlefields are reefs.

*Designers:* a property of belt chunks rather than a new entity: rimed chunks spit spicules when mined and yield lattice.

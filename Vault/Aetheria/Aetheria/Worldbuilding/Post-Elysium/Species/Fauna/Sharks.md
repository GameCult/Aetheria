**"A shark that won't hunt is a ship."**

> Draft, 2026-10-13. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Sharks are the size of a light ship: long, dark, with a ridge of heat-drinking tissue along the back that glows dull red after a kill. They hunt alone or in twos and threes, and they hunt by heat.

#### What They Eat

Minnows, mostly, and anything else warm. A shark's heat-organ pulls warmth out of prey the way krill pull it out of sunlight, only faster [new]. A reactor is the warmest thing in a system, and sharks know it.

#### How They Move: the Tell

Sharks cut across the orbital flow. They patrol the steep side of a well in long passes, then fall in toward their prey, using the planet's pull to sprint. They accelerate harder than anything else alive and change heading without warning.

**What a pilot learns:** a shark is a ship-sized return that ignores the orbit, which is exactly what a ship looks like. The difference is behaviour: sharks patrol, then strike downhill, then patrol again. They never loiter, never hold station, and never fly the same pass twice. A shark-sized return that sits still, or climbs out of a well at full burn, or doesn't go for a bait ball, is a ship. Sharks are the best disguise for a fighter and the hardest to keep up.

#### How They Fight

- **The bite.** A sprinting shark rams and latches, tearing armour [new].
- **The drain.** A latched shark drinks heat. It cools the ship it holds and steals its energy, and it lets go when the ship goes cold or the shark is full [new].
- **Heat-seeking.** Sharks go for the hottest thing nearby. A ship running hot draws them. A ship disguised inside a krill cloud and running hot draws them faster, and the sharks give it away.
- **Running cold is defence.** Shut down, go dark, and a shark loses interest.

#### Harvest

*Shark hunting* is bounty work, done by pilots who can survive the bite. Yields heat-organs, the best radiators money can buy, and hide, which [[Zhestokost]] layers into armour [new]. Sharks are also a weapon: leading a pack into a rival's krill grounds to scatter the schools and scare off rakers is a recognized form of [[Resource Denial]] [new].

#### Where They Live

On the steep inner side of wells, above minnow schools. Wherever there are minnows there are sharks, and wherever sharks circle a krill cloud, something warm is inside it.

#### For Designers

- Motion law: cross-flow patrol, then a downhill sprint, then patrol; hardest acceleration of any class.
- Hiding: the fighter's disguise; the tell is loitering, holding station or climbing out of a well.
- Hazard: ram damage, latch-and-drain (heat and energy), attracted to heat signature.
- Harvest: heat-organs (radiators), hide (armour); bounty work.
- Placement: steep side of wells, near minnow schools; can be lured onto a target.

**"A shark that won't hunt is a ship."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: pursuit predator. Kept from the operator's sketch and r1. Changed: sharks hunt the shear lines at well shoulders, the way albatrosses fly wind shear, and they are middle predators, not the top of the web. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

The size of a light ship: long, dark, with a ridge of heat-drinking tissue along the back that glows dull red after a kill. Alone or in twos and threes.

**Eats.** [[Minnows]], and anything warm. In a sea where heat is food, a reactor is the richest smell there is.

**Moves: the tell.** Sharks never drift and never stop. They soar the shear where a well's swirl meets the open current, gaining speed for free on every pass, then cut out of it and fall downslope onto prey. They accelerate harder than anything else alive.

**What a pilot learns.** A shark is a ship-sized return that ignores the current, which is what a ship looks like. The difference is behaviour: patrol the shear, strike downhill, patrol again. A shark-sized return that holds station, climbs out of a well at full burn, or ignores a bait ball is a ship.

**On sensors.** A ship-sized return below identification. Warm after it feeds.

**How it hurts.** The bite: a sprinting shark rams and latches, tearing armour. The drain: a latched shark drinks heat, cooling the ship and stealing its energy until it is full or the ship is cold [new]. A drained ship reads colder than its surroundings, and spacers say a contact that cold is dead, Adrasteian, or bitten. Running cold is the defence: shut down and a shark loses interest.

**Yield.** Bounty work. Heat-organs make the best radiators money can buy; hide is layered into armour by [[Zhestokost]] [new]. Sharks are also a weapon: a pack led into a rival's krill grounds is [[Resource Denial]].

**Lives.** The shoulders of wells, above minnow schools. Wherever sharks circle a krill cloud, something warm is inside it.

*Designers:* anchor moving across the flow; patrol on the shear, downhill sprint; attracted by heat; hazards are ram, latch and drain.

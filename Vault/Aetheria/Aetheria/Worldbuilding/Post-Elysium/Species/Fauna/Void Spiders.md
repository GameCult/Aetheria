**"The net between the moons isn't ours."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: trap predator. From the operator's 2020 line about "space whales and space spiders", named as a gap in r1 and filled here. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Many-limbed, the size of a fighter, almost all reach. A void spider strings a net of fine glittering silk across slack water and waits at its hub. The nets are kilometres wide and nearly invisible until something is caught in them.

**Eats.** Whatever drifts or swims into the net: krill rafts stalled in the slack, minnows, the occasional shark. Drift piles up in slack water; spiders sit where the current delivers.

**Moves: the tell.** The spider doesn't, until something hits the net; then it runs the strands to it, fast.

**What a pilot learns.** Krill that have stopped drifting in a neat sheet, in slack water, are caught. Glints in a plane where no plane should be are silk. A spider at the hub is a small warm return that does not move with anything.

**On sensors.** The net barely shows on active and not at all on passive; the spider is a small stationary contact.

**How it hurts.** The snare: silk sticks to hulls, slowing and tethering a ship that flies into a net; heavy ships tear free, light ones are held [new]. A held ship is bitten and drained like a shark's prey, but slowly.

**Yield.** Silk, the strongest fibre in Elysium: tethers, cargo nets, armour weave. *Silking*, cutting nets whole while the spider is away or dead, is skilled and well paid.

**Lives.** Saddles and trojan points between bodies, and across convergence fronts where the current runs slow.

*Designers:* anchor at a slack point with a net area; hazard is snare (slow and tether) then drain.

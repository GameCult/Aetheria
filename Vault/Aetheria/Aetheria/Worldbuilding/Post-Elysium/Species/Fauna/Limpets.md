**"Scrape the limpets or wear a lamp."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: parasite. New in r2. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Palm-sized discs with a sucking foot and a back of membrane. Limpets drift until they meet something warm, then fasten and drink its heat. Their natural hosts are [[Whales]], which carry thousands. Ships are warm enough [new].

**Eats.** Heat, through the hull.

**Moves: the tell.** Drifts with the current while free; rides its host after that.

**What a pilot learns.** A limpet colony is a lamp: limpets glow faintly as they feed, so a crusted ship is brighter than it should be. A hunter who sees a cluster of small glowing returns in a fixed pattern, moving together and ignoring the current, has found a hull.

**On sensors.** A crusted ship's signature is raised; that is the whole harm and the whole tell.

**How it hurts.** A slow drain: heat and a little energy, and a raised signature that ruins stealth until they are scraped off. Running hot through drifting limpets, or loitering near a whale, picks them up [new].

**Yield.** Little. Scraping is a dock service. Limpet foot glue is sold as a hull sealant.

**Lives.** Around whales, in thick glim, and wherever warm ships loiter.

*Designers:* a status on the ship rather than a contact: signature up, small heat drain, removed at dock or by burning hot.

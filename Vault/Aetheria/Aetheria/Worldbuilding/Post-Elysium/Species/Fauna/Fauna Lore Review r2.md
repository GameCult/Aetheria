#### Where I'd Change Things

**1. Strange matter is grown, in whale bone. (Existing canon: [[Negative Entropy]], Running Cold, Adrasteia.)** *Ruled 2026-10-14: not biogenic (`strange-matter-not-biogenic`). Cut from the notes in r3; strange matter is stellar dust of the Ink, concentrated by its animals ([[Adrasteia Space]], [[Fauna Lore Review r3]]). The argument below is kept as the record of what was proposed.*
Canon says negent needs strange matter found only in Adrasteia-controlled pseudospace, that only Adrasteia makes the charged form, and that its supplier's location is unknown. I'd give that a cause: strange matter is laid down slowly in the bones of [[Whales]], and Adrasteia's "controlled pseudospace" is a field of ancient falls, a bone bed it mines the way Earth mined fossil plankton as oil. Every canon sentence stays true. What it buys: a reason harvest is big in Elysium, a three-way conflict over every whale road (Adrasteia buying bone in secret, the [[Cetacean Navigators]] forbidding whaling, independents in the middle), and a threat to the monopoly every time a whale dies outside Adrasteia space. What it costs: some of Adrasteia's mystery, which their brief says should be about who makes the machinery and how it works. The notes as written keep it a rumour among flensers, which is my recommendation: the value is real, the explanation unproven. This is a question in the mind (`aetheria-fauna:question:strange-matter-source`).

**2. The fauna ride the Aether current, not the orbit; and the nebula is partly alive. (First pass; the Sol sketch.)**
r1 gave krill the orbital flow as their motion law. The flow the game actually rules on (`flow-moves-what-ruled`, map B15) is a braided global current plus a swirl around each well and a drain downslope, and it is what the nebula and stardust already follow. So the tell is "drifts with the current" and the flying aid is a current-sync, not an orbit-sync. Once the current is the medium, the ecology falls out of its shape: fronts where it converges, shear at well shoulders, slack in saddles, debris spiralling into wells. Making the glowing part of the nebula alive ([[Glim]]) gives the web its producers, which r1 lacked: krill are grazers, not plants. It also gives your cloudless Sol a meaning: Sol has no clouds because nothing there is alive. One tension: canon calls the Aether a *static reference frame*. I'd keep the frame static for drives and say the current is motion in the Aetheric field, or reword the Aether note; either is a one-line canon edit.

**3. The fauna are residents, not arrivals; they refill from upstream. (Your origin sketch, the first pass, [[Pseudospace]].)**
r1 had fauna pour in through wormhole mouths in a tide. Canon's Pseudospace note says the opposite of an incursion: flat space is the stranger here. I'd keep your sketch's fact, that humanity first met them after opening wormholes, and drop the arrival: the wormholes let humanity out into a sea that was already full. Respawn then has a natural cause, larvae carried in on the current from deeper pseudospace, which is how coral reefs repopulate. Two things follow: bigger animals the deeper you go has a cause (stronger current, thicker glim), and recovery can scale with trophic level. Krill back in days, whales in years. That gives harvest stakes without breaking `fauna-harvest-ruled`'s respawn.

**4. Your four were a fine start and the wrong floor.** Kept all four, changed each: [[Krill]] graze glim; [[Minnows]] are the forage layer; [[Sharks]] are middle predators that hunt the shear, not the apex; [[Whales]] ride gravity waves, which is why they arc. The niches they did not cover are now filled: producer ([[Glim]]), network ([[Skein]]), reef ([[Rime]]), ambush ([[Lanterns]]), trap ([[Void Spiders]]), scavenger ([[Gleaners]]), parasite ([[Limpets]]), mind-grazer ([[Moths]]). The apex is left empty on purpose: the thing that eats whales lives past the charts.

**5. "The fauna are honest" was the wrong rule.** r1 said nothing in the ecology mimics anything, to protect the disguise game. Nature's best tricks are mimicry, and the rule that matters is the sim's, not the animals': no fauna writes a false belief (`fauna-never-lie`). A [[Lanterns|lantern]] emits real heat that reads like a crippled ship. That keeps the invariant and adds the hazard of the false wreck.

**6. Sol's edge is a shoreline.** Keep Sol cloudless and lifeless inside. At the Oort edge, the current breaks against flat space and drops what it carries: a wrack line of dead krill, moth husks and rimed comets. That puts the sea where a Sol-born pilot first sees it, before the first jump, and gives salvagers a beach. If strandings are dead loose matter rather than a fauna field, `fauna-none-in-sol` holds unchanged. Optional.

**7. Wild negent.** Canon frames negent as a powerful conversion that needs strange matter. I'd add that the Aether does it weakly everywhere, unaided: warm things glow faintly in a band life can eat. That one sentence is the whole energy budget of pseudospace and the reason the fauna come to the guns.

**8. r1's "living concrete" for Blooms contradicts [[Bloom]] canon** (processed rubble, heavy industry). Dropped. [[Rime]] binding rubble piles is the version that fits.

#### Left Open on Purpose

- **Are they the garden?** Canon calls Elysium a garden that serves as a prison. Whether the fauna are wild life of pseudospace or part of the Timeless' garden is not answered here. The notes describe them as wild; nothing rules out the other. Worth deciding before anyone writes the edge giants.
- **Other species.** The Ratfolk perceive by vibration and would know the fauna differently: names, uses, fears. Worth a pass by whoever owns the Ratfolk.

**"If the moths are thick, someone is lying on the Aethernet."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: mind-grazer. New in r2. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Pale, flickering things the size of a hand, with wide, soft, dusted membranes. The [[Aether]] is attuned to cognition: minds and information bend it ([[Cognitive Interfacing]]). Moths feed on that bend. They are drawn to crews, ship minds, [[Aethernet]] transmitters and stations, and to [[Spirits]], which they eat until nothing is left [new].

**Eats.** Mind-light: the Aether disturbance around thinking things and around signal. Loud transmitters, AGI cores, [[Necrotech]] and espers are feasts.

**Moves: the tell.** Drift with the current until they sense a mind, then flutter toward it in a cloud that thickens around the brightest source.

**What a pilot learns.** Moths gather where someone is transmitting or thinking hard. A moth cloud with no contact in it is a ship running dark but talking. Necrotech hulls attract them in swarms, which is the dissidents' favourite argument that bound spirits are minds.

**On sensors.** A soft cloud of tiny returns, centred on a source.

**How it hurts.** They eat signal: a thick cloud garbles comms and Aethernet traffic around its host [new]. Espers inside a moth cloud report a pressure behind the eyes and lose focus. Bound spirits in necrotech degrade faster.

**Yield.** Moth dust, the scales from their wings. A pinch lets a non-esper brush the Aether for a few minutes. It is illegal everywhere, sold everywhere, and corporate media call it a hallucinogen, as they call espers hallucinating [new].

**Lives.** Around stations, relays and busy lanes; thinly everywhere else. They cannot live in Sol, which has no current to carry them, and which is the brightest mind-light in Elysium. Spacers say moths dream of it.

*Designers:* field drawn to transmitters and minds; hazard is comms degradation; reveals transmitting ships.

**"Never kill the first whale you see on a run."**

> Draft, 2026-10-13. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Whales are many times the size of any ship: slow, warm, ribbed with pale bone, and trailing a veil of feeding membrane wide enough to sweep a krill cloud whole. A whale glows in thermal like a small station. It sings, and the song carries through the [[Aether]].

#### What They Eat

Krill, in bulk. A whale's arc runs through the biggest clouds around a gas giant and leaves a clean channel behind it, wider and softer-edged than a trawler's.

#### How They Move: the Tell

Whales swing in long, slow arcs between a gas giant and its moons, taking hours or days to complete one. They never hurry, never turn sharply, and repeat their arcs closely enough that [[Cetacean Navigators]] chart them as whale roads [new].

**What a pilot learns:** a whale is the brightest thing in its sky and the slowest. Nothing a ship can do looks like a whale except flying that arc for a very long time. A ship near a whale is hard to separate from it, which makes hanging in a whale's shadow tempting, and dangerous.

#### How They Fight

Whales don't hunt anything that isn't krill. They defend.

- **The turn.** A whale that is hurt or crowded rolls. Its mass and membrane sweep anything close: ships are battered, krill and minnows scattered [new].
- **The breach.** A threatened whale dumps its stored heat in a thermal flare that washes out thermal sensors for a long way around [new].
- **The song.** A distressed whale sings loud. The song runs through the [[Aether]] and floods the [[Aethernet]] locally, drowning nearby traffic in noise. It is truth, not deception: everyone in range knows a whale is in trouble, and roughly where [new].

#### Harvest

*Flensing.* A whale yields bone that carries traces of the strange matter [[Negative Entropy]] needs, bought quietly by Adrasteia [new]; oil that runs reactors cold and long; and membrane prized for fine nets. A whale kill is the richest single harvest in Elysium and the most contested.

A dead whale is a *fall*. The carcass drifts for weeks on its last orbit, and everything comes to it: minnows, sharks, rakers, flensers, pirates, Navigators. A fall is an event, a temporary hotspot that redraws the local ecology.

#### The Slow Singers

The [[Cetacean Navigators]] don't call them whales; their name for them translates as *the slow singers* [new]. Their position is that the question of whether the slow singers are persons is open, and in a setting where personhood is disputed and owned, that is a political stance. Navigator space allows no whaling, only salvage from natural falls [new]. [[Esper]]s say the song has structure. The Queen's Guard says it has bandwidth [new].

#### Where They Live

Around gas giants, on broad, gentle slopes where the krill clouds are biggest, in arcs that reach out to the moons. Rarely around large rocky planets. Never sunward.

#### For Designers

- Motion law: slow, long, repeating arcs; very bright thermal.
- Hiding: shadowing a whale (later); disguising as one requires flying its arc.
- Hazard: roll (area damage and scatter), thermal flare (sensor wash), song (local Aethernet noise).
- Harvest: bone (strange-matter trace), oil, membrane; very high value; kills produce a fall event.
- Placement: gas giants and their moons' orbits; their passage leaves channels in krill.

**"The skein knows where the food is. Follow it."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: network organism, neither animal nor plant. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

The skein is one organism the size of a system: a web of faint threads joining well to well, thickening where food is and fading where it isn't, rebuilding itself as the bodies move. It behaves the way slime mould does on a dish: it grows toward food, reinforces the paths that carry the most, and lets the rest wither, until the web is a map of the zone's gravity and its plenty [new].

**Eats.** Nothing directly. It moves food. Glim caught on its strands is passed along them toward the wells that need it; the threads pulse faintly when they carry.

**Moves.** It grows. Strands run along the gravity slope, around the shoulders of wells and across the saddles between them, and shift as the bodies orbit. It never drifts; it is anchored to the slope, not the current.

**What a pilot sees.** Threads of pale light linking bodies, brightest along the busiest paths. Krill and minnows string out along it like beads.

**On sensors.** A faint lattice on passive; nothing on active. It is never a contact.

**How it hurts.** It doesn't. It is the one thing in Open Water that never attacks. Flying through it breaks strands, which regrow; the [[Krill]] scatter.

**Yield.** Thread, by the gram: it carries Aether disturbance along its length, and makes fine antenna for [[Aethernet]] gear. Cetacean Navigators consider harvesting it vandalism of the chart [new].

**Lives.** Every flatspace zone outside Sol, anchored to the slope. Not in the Ink, where there is no glim to carry; there the seams between two pillars' reaches, where their waves cross and their dust piles up, are the nearest thing to strands [new]. Deep zones have skeins as thick as cables.

*Designers:* the skein is the slime-mould pattern the placement ruling asks for, given a body in the lore. It is drawn from the gravity slope at run time, as the placement law already is. The ambient slime trails could be it.

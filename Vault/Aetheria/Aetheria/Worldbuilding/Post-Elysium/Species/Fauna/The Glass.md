**"In the Glass you don't hide. You decide what you were seen doing."**

> Draft lore pass r4, 2026-10-14, for the operator's review. A layer of [[Wonderland]], first sketched in r3 and now proposed as the Wavecrafters' pseudospace home. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon; everything about the Wavecrafters here is [new] beyond canon's [[Wavecrafters]] note. #### What It Looks Like

Through the link the current stops. The slope goes still enough to be a mirror. The nebula hangs where it has always hung, sharp-edged, and the stars are needle points at distances that should have blurred them. You can see everything in the Glass, very far away, and everything can see you.

Behind your ship a line stays on the slope: your wake, printed into the still Aether, fading over hours instead of seconds. Look around and there are other lines, old and older, crossing like the tracks of skaters on a frozen lake. Some are yours. Most are not.

**The map.** The Glass's galaxy map is the sharpest in the game: silver on black, every zone visible at once, nothing in fog. Drawn over it are the wakes: recent traffic as bright lines, older as faint ones. The map tells you where everyone is, and where everyone has been [new].

#### How It Works

- **Stiff Aether.** No current. Nothing drifts, nothing mixes, nothing is carried. Whatever is left somewhere stays there.
- **Heat stays.** The Aether makes no light of heat here (wild [[Negative Entropy]] never happens), so a hull's heat has almost nowhere to go. Radiators stall; ships climb toward their limits; a battle in the Glass is short [new].
- **Wakes persist.** Every disturbance a hull makes in the Aether stays printed on it: course, speed, the drive's signature, the moment a weapon fired. A wake is a record no one can edit.
- **Light carries.** Nothing eats light and nothing scatters it. Sensors reach further here than anywhere.

#### Who Lives There

The **Wavecrafters**. Canon makes them the people who know how disturbances travel, how systems are observed, and how machinery couples to the field, and says their inner practice is hidden and their senior cultivators travel through "protected guild channels". This note proposes where both come from [new]:

- **The Glass is their school.** A place where every disturbance stays is the only place you can watch one travel from start to finish. Apprentices learn *listening* by sitting beside a wake and reading it for days. The breath forms began as ways to manage heat in a place that keeps it.
- **The Glass is their secret's reason.** In the Glass nothing can be hidden, and the Wavecrafters learned there what being seen costs. Their concealment of espers in flatspace is the discipline of people who grew up where concealment was impossible: once you leave the Glass, you never again let yourself be read that easily.
- **The protected channels are Glass links.** Guild houses keep the links, and the far ends are guild land. A Wavecrafter master travelling the Glass can see a pursuer coming for days, and a pursuer can see where she went, which is why the guild keeps the links rather than the roads.
- **Sacred Instruments are made here** more than anywhere: an instrument cultivated for years in a place where every disturbance stays can learn its relation exactly.

#### What It Does to People

The Glass takes privacy. Crews who stay too long feel watched for the rest of their lives, in rooms with no windows, and they cannot forget: memory sets like the wakes. It gives sight of how things travel: returners hear a faint signal under noise, see where a stranger has been from how she stands, and some of them, the ones who stayed longest, come back espers. The guild calls them its own, whether or not they agree [new].

#### Its Animals

Everything alive in the Glass is slow, thin and old, because a body makes heat and here heat cannot leave. Only life with a vast surface and a tiny metabolism survives [new].

| Niche | Animal | One line |
|---|---|---|
| Producer | **Baskets** | Lattices kilometres across, grown over centuries, catching the Glass's long light; nothing kills them, and very little grows |
| Grazer | **Skaters** | Flat, fast, short-lived things that ride fresh wakes and feed on the disturbance itself; they follow ships the way gulls follow a trawler |
| Predator | **Augurs** | Still hunters that read wakes and wait where a wake will be. They never chase; they are already there |

Baskets are the Glass's oldest landmarks; some are on Wavecrafter maps by name. Skaters are a pilot's tell that a wake is fresh: if skaters are on it, someone passed within the hour. An augur waiting ahead of you means you have been flying predictably.

Spacers say: *every wake is a confession*; *if the skaters are behind you, so is someone else*; and of a Wavecrafter, *she's already read you*.

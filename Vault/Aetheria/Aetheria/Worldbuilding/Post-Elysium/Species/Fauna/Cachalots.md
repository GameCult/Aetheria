**"Nobody hunts cachalots. People follow them, and pick up the grey."**

> Draft r3, 2026-10-14, revised r6. Part of [[Adrasteia Space]] (the Ink); niche: apex. Named for the sperm whale, the Earth animal that dives into the dark after giant squid. Not related to the [[Whales]] of Open Water, and spacers who confuse them don't do it twice. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

The largest thing in the Ink: a blunt, scarred head, a body many times a ship's length, skin the colour of the fog. Cachalots keep the two edges of the cradle, the break and the bank, and go under the surface between, rising into a pocket only to take the warmth, or to kill.

**Eats.** [[Squid]], and the dust they carry. Every squid a cachalot eats adds its dust to the cachalot's. That is where the Ink's dust ends up: at the top of the web, in a few hundred animals near each pillar [new].

**Moves: the tell.** Long, deep dives under the surface and into the bank, out of every sensor's reach, and slow rises at the break, where each new wave comes up. They keep the pillar's long breath, not its beat, and ride no waves [new]. When a cachalot rises, the squid in that pocket scatter.

**What a pilot learns.** A cachalot does not need to see you. It holds so much dust that it eats heat at a range no other animal can, and it hunts squid by their heat in the bank, where nothing can see anything. To a cachalot a ship is a very warm squid.

**On sensors.** Usually nothing: it is in the clouds. When it rises, a cold so large the board reads a hole. Then, if it feeds, the flare.

**How it hurts.** *The pull*: heat drained from a ship at long range, faster than any squid can manage. *The lance*: a cachalot that has eaten more heat than it can hold throws it off as a beam of hard light, the negent conversion done all at once. It is the brightest thing in the Ink after a pillar, and it is what spacers mean when they say a ship was *lit* [new]. *The ram*, for anything that has made it angry.

**Yield.** *Grey*: like the ambergris of Earth's sperm whales, a waxy concretion cachalots pass, built around the indigestible beaks of the squid they eat and dense with dust. It drifts out of the inner edge of the bank into the passing pockets, and grey-pickers trail cachalots for weeks to find it. Grey holds more dust than anything else a living crew can take. That a cachalot's body holds more still is common knowledge. Nobody sells any [new].

**Lives.** The break and the bank of each pillar, and the deep water under the cradle between them. The foot, where the dust is thickest and every wave begins, lies under the break, and cachalots come up from somewhere near it. They are why nobody reaches the foot of a pillar.

*Designers:* a rare anchor near pillars that rises into pockets on a long cycle; a very long-range heat drain; a heat-threshold beam attack; a grey drop that can be collected without a kill. Treat a kill as out of reach unless a later ruling makes it a quest.

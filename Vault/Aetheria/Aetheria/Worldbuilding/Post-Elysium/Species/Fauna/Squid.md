**"If the radiators are happy, be afraid."**

> Draft r3, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Adrasteia Space]] (the Ink); niche: pack predator. Named for the Earth animal that hunts in the dark and leaves ink behind it. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Long, pale, many-armed, the size of a light ship, and almost never seen: they live in the fog walls at the edges of pockets and in the inky clouds, and come into the clear only to strike. Packs of three to a dozen.

**Eats.** [[Rays]], other squid, and heat. A squid holds enough dust in its body to eat heat at a distance: it can drain a ray, or a ship, from inside the fog without touching it [new].

**Moves: the tell.** Squid hunt the pocket edge. They pace a pocket from inside its fog wall, just out of the light, and strike inward as a pack when something comes close to the wall. They do not ride the swell; they hold their place against it. Between waves they sink into the inky clouds.

**What a pilot learns.** Stay in the middle of the pocket. Never ride the wall. When the pocket closes, the squid are already where you are. A pack hunts heat, so the warmest ship in a pocket is the one they take.

**On sensors.** A cold contact that grows colder as it closes, because it is drinking your heat at range. Several at the pocket edge make the edge itself read cold. Squid are why Ink crews call any contact colder than the fog a squid until it proves otherwise.

**How it hurts.** *The hush*: a pack drinks heat at range and a ship goes quiet. Systems slow, weapons stall, and the crew grows calm, the danger [[Running Cold]] warns of, done by an animal. *The strike*: arms and beak, close in, tearing armour. *Ink*: a wounded or retreating squid clouds the water with a dust-heavy dark that eats light and sensor returns alike and lingers after the pocket has passed. An inked ship is blind until it flies clear.

**Yield.** *Ink sacs*: dust in fine suspension, more concentrated than any ray's plates. The richest harvest a crew can take and usually live. Old squid ink is also a smuggler's paint: a hull coated in it is very hard to see anywhere [new].

**Lives.** The fog walls of pockets near the pillars, and the inky clouds, which their ink helps to thicken.

*Designers:* a pack of anchors pacing a moving pocket from outside its edge; strike when a heat source nears the wall; hazards are a ranged heat drain, melee, and a persistent sensor-occluding ink cloud; slow respawn.

#### What r4 Did

One frame for the whole set:

- **[[Flatspace]]**: Sol's nebula-free bubble plus the vanilla galaxy. Strange physics a crew can learn; the ordinary factions; [[Open Water]] (r2's ecology, renamed from "Pseudospace Ecology") as its natural wildlife, thin in the core and rich at the edges where the Chaos factions live; pirates as its scourge.
- **[[Wonderland]]** (replaces "Pseudospace Flavours"): separate **layers**, each its own space and its own map, joined by **links** that are found, have tells, sometimes keep hours, and are held by being known. Depth counted in links and time. The fae register: each layer takes something and gives something, and travellers' tales are how layers become known.
- **Four layers**, each with its map's look, foothold, dangers, what it does to people, and animals: [[Adrasteia Space|the Ink]] (Adrasteia), [[The Glass]] (the Wavecrafters), [[The Fold]] (nobody; the Undertow and the Parallax Auditors work it), [[Hyperspace]] (the beacon keepers), with the beacon economy.
- Links and wording fixed across the fauna notes so nothing still says the fauna live "in pseudospace".

#### Where I'd Change Your Decisions

**1. Hyperspace should be indifferent, not malevolent.** "Like the Warp" is right about the stakes and I wrote it that way: without beacons you do not get out. But the Warp is hostile; it is full of things that want you. I'd keep hyperspace's danger in its geometry: errors grow exponentially, signals die exponentially, there is too much room to be found in. Then the stakes come from Aetheria's own systems (sensing, heat, drive wear, insurance, EW) rather than from an antagonist, which is your point against HyperRogue. The uncanny is in the people (the Lost) and two animals that exploit lost ships, not in the place.

**2. "Connect arbitrarily" should be arbitrary to the player, structured in the world.** If links are uniformly random, nothing about them can be learned, and a pilot's knowledge stops mattering. I'd keep them unpredictable but give them a grammar: most open at flatspace's edges; links leak tells of their far side; some open on a beat; and they are owned by whoever knows them. That keeps navigation exciting and makes link charts the most valuable thing in the game.

**3. "Some fauna" in flatspace.** r2's twelve animals are more than "some". I kept them and made density do the work: almost nothing in Sol, thin in the settled core, rich at the edges. If you meant flatspace to be nearly empty, r2's ecology moves outward wholesale and the core loses its krill; that is one paragraph in [[Flatspace]].

#### Where I'd Change Self's Proposals

**1. Hyperspace heat.** Self proposed "heat with nowhere to go". Hyperbolic space has more room to radiate into, not less, so that reasoning runs backwards. I moved the heat to the drive: coupling to hyperspace is a transition that never finishes, and holding it open makes heat and wear for the whole crossing. That also ties hyperspace to canon's Wavecrafter line about carrying a drive through a difficult transition, and makes an Undertow calibration profile the commonest way ships are lost.

**2. Beacons need a reason to be dense.** Self's beacon economy holds. I added the physics that makes it an economy: a signal spreads into exponentially more room, so it fades exponentially, so you can only ever hear the next beacon. Chains must be dense, each beacon is a crew's work, and keepers are the people who live in hyperspace, which answers your "who lives in hyperspace?".

**3. The Fold has traffic, not a foothold.** A foothold is held by keeping links secret, and in the Fold every seam can be seen from its far side. So nobody holds it, the Undertow runs off-ledger routes through it, and the Parallax Auditors live off the contradictory records it produces. Self's other calls (flatspace's ecology from r2, layers not flavours, Wonderland as the collective name, the Glass for the Wavecrafters, the Fold distinct from hyperspace, false beacons as an EW claim, fauna homing on lost ships) are in the notes as proposed.

#### Where I'd Change r3

**1. The Ink question is half-answered by layers.** Question `aetheria-fauna:question:ink-one-or-many` asked whether there is one Ink or many. With layers each having one map, the Ink is naturally one layer with many pillars, reached by several links. What is left is whether every link into it is Adrasteia's. I'd have Self revise the question to that, or withdraw it; the notes are written so that both answers fit.

**2. Margins become links.** r3's flavours met at margins a few minutes wide. Layers do not border each other, so r3's border tells now live at links, where a link leaks its far side.

**3. Flatspace is not "no flavour".** r3 called flat space the absence of a flavour. It has its own answers to the five dials (light carries, heat leaves as light, the slope holds, a braided current, a kilometre stays a kilometre), and [[Wonderland]] shows them as the baseline every layer departs from.

#### Where I'd Change Canon

**1. [[Pseudospace]] gives two causes for flatness, and one is the best idea in the set.** Canon says "we brought flat space with us; we are the incursion". [[Temporal Nonlinearity]] says Elysium is a shard of spacetime the Timeless built. Canon also says the Aether answers to minds. Put together: flatspace may be held flat by the minds that expect it, which would explain Sol's bubble (the oldest, most crowded expectation), why Wonderland changes people (too few of you to hold the rules), and why Flatlanders and Chaos factions fight about it. It is a cosmology fork, so it is question `aetheria-fauna:question:why-flatspace-is-flat`. [[Flatspace]] gives all three accounts in-world and rules nothing.

**2. "Few islands of stability".** The vanilla map is large. I'd keep canon's line and add that the islands look like a continent from inside; "few" is the view from Wonderland.

**3. [[Wavecrafters]]: one line for the Glass.** Their "protected guild channels" become Glass links, their school is the Glass, and their concealment of espers is what people raised where nothing hides learn to value. All [new], all consistent with the canon text.

**4. The Cetacean Navigators are a Pre-Elysium faction.** r2 and r4 both carry them into Elysium (r2 as current-readers, r4 as the root of the keepers' rescue duty). Nothing I read says they survived the Rupture. Someone should check before that line goes further.

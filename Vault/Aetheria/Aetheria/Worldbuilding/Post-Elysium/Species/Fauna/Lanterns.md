**"Never answer a light in the slack."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: ambush predator, mimic. New in r2. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

A lantern is a mouth with a light in front of it, the size of a gunship, almost all of it dark. The light is a lure organ that pulses warm and uneven, the way a crippled reactor does [new]. Deep-sea anglerfish hunt this way; so do lanterns.

**Eats.** [[Sharks]], mostly, which come to warm things, and the occasional salvager, which also does.

**Moves: the tell.** It doesn't. A lantern settles in slack water, the saddles between wells where the current goes still, and holds there for weeks. Its lure pulses on an irregular rhythm.

**What a pilot learns.** A warm, ship-sized return that holds still and flickers looks exactly like a disabled ship, and a disabled ship looks like salvage or a distress call. That is the point. Tells: it sits in slack water and nowhere else; nothing small lives near it; and sharks that should be circling it are missing. Real wrecks drift. Lanterns don't.

**On sensors.** A ship-sized warm contact, stationary, thermal flickering. Below identification it is indistinguishable from a crippled hull. It does not lie: its heat is real heat. It is the reading that misleads.

**How it hurts.** The strike: anything that closes to within reach is engulfed, held and crushed, heavy hull damage, and the lantern does not let go until it is hurt badly enough [new].

**Yield.** The lure organ, which makes thermal decoys that pulse like a damaged reactor; pirates use them to fake distress [new]. Lantern hide is dark enough to sell to anyone who runs cold.

**Lives.** Saddles and slack points between bodies, often near a [[Void Spiders|spider]] net. Rare, one to a saddle.

*Designers:* anchor that holds station; the mimic is a real emission, so it fits the rule that fauna never write false beliefs. A hazard that rewards the hunter's patience and punishes the rescuer's haste.

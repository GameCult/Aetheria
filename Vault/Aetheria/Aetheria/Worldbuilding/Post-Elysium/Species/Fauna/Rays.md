**"Rays ride out. If one is coming in, something is chasing it."**

> Draft r3, 2026-10-14, revised r6. Part of [[Adrasteia Space]] (the Ink); niche: bulk grazer that surfs the swell. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Black wings as wide as a frigate, thin as sailcloth, rising on the swell. A ray surfaces in a pocket with its whole width spread flat on the water, skimming, and the dust caught in the plates of its mouth flickers when the light finds it.

**Eats.** [[Soot]] and wave dust, skimmed off the surface through broad plates like a manta's gill rakers. The dust stays in those plates for life and builds up [new].

**Moves: the tell.** Rays surf. Each one catches a wave near the pillar, rides it outward inside its pocket, grazing, and slips off the back as the pocket weakens; then it slides back down the slope of the well under the fog to catch the next. A ray's whole life is that commute, out on the beat and back between [new]. They never swim against a wave. A ray going inward through a pocket is fleeing.

**What a pilot learns.** Rays are the Ink's weather vane. Where they surf, the pocket is safe and the beat is steady. A school breaking off the wave all at once means [[Squid]] in the pocket wall.

**On sensors.** Wide, flat, cold returns riding a wave front, close to the surface. A school reads as one long cold smear moving at the speed of the swell.

**How it hurts.** It defends; it doesn't hunt. A ray's wing drinks heat from whatever is beneath it, and a startled school can blanket a ship, chilling it fast and blinding its sensors for as long as they cover it. A diving ray's wingbeat throws a short, steep swell.

**Yield.** *Plates*: the mouth plates hold more dust than soot ever does, in a form that keeps. Ray hunting is the Ink's mid-grade trade: well paid, risky, and the reason [[Squid]] learn to follow skimmer crews. *Wing*: a cold-holding fabric prized for insulation.

**Lives.** The cradle, on the swell, between the break and the bank. Thickest in the inner cradle, where the soot is freshest; never in the break, where the swell is steep enough to roll them.

*Designers:* anchor moving outward with the pulse and returning under fog between pulses; flees inward when threatened; hazards are a heat-drain blanket and sensor occlusion; moderate respawn.

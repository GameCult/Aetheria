**"Never trust a straight line in the Fold. If it matches you, it is you."**

> Draft lore pass r4, 2026-10-14, for the operator's review. A layer of [[Wonderland]], from r3's flavour of the same name. It is where canon's definition of [[Pseudospace]], space that does not follow Euclid, is true in the most local way: distance itself bends. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon. Reasoning in [[Fauna Lore Review r4]].

#### What It Looks Like

Through the link the stars double. Some smear into arcs, some sit in the wrong place, and one or two appear twice in different parts of the sky. The slope is solid and the current runs, so at first it feels like flatspace. Then the ranges on your board stop agreeing with each other, and a straight course starts to curve.

**The map.** The Fold's galaxy map cannot be drawn flat, so it is drawn honestly: some zones appear twice, in different places; some edges are marked as glued to edges far across the map; and the lines between zones are labelled with depth, not distance. Pilots fly it by the labels and ignore the picture [new].

#### How It Flies

- **Knots.** Near a knot a kilometre grows longer the deeper in you go, so a moon's neighbourhood is bigger inside than it looks from outside. Fleets hide inside knots. A pursuer who chases you into one finds the space unfolding ahead faster than either of you can cross it.
- **Seams.** Along a seam two far places are glued edge to edge. Fly through and you are somewhere far away; look back and you see where you came from, small and close. A seam is a link inside the layer, and the Fold is full of them.
- **Sight lines bend.** Light curves around a knot, so you can see a contact that no straight shot can reach, and from two directions at once. Beams follow the light; slugs and missiles follow their own, different curve. Learning the difference is learning to fight here.
- **Ranges lie, honestly.** Range on sensors is the light's path, the truth about the light and not about your trip. Pilots fly by **depth**, how far into a knot they are, measured against the [[Aether]]'s reference frame.
- **You meet yourself.** Light can go round a knot and come back, so a ship deep in the Fold sometimes sees its own image ahead of it, minutes old. It is real light: it is where you were.

#### Who Holds It

Nobody. A foothold is held by holding the anchors at the far ends of its links ([[Anchors and Gates]]). An anchor in the Fold is in two places, as anything there can be, and a gate opened on it may open at either; every gate into the Fold is half blind, and nobody's has stayed theirs for long. And the far end of every seam can be seen from it, so nothing inside the Fold is secret either. What the Fold has instead is traffic [new]:

- **The Undertow** (canon's off-ledger retuners and smugglers) runs routes through Fold seams that no registry can follow, because the route on the record and the route flown are not the same length.
- **The [[Parallax Auditors]]** make a living here. A ship that crossed the Fold may have been seen in two places at once, by two honest sensors. That is continuity residue in canon's exact sense, and the Fold makes more of it than anywhere.

#### What It Does to People

The Fold takes certainty about who came back. A crew that has met its own image enough times starts to wonder which ship turned home. The tales are changeling tales: the one who returned is kind where she was sharp, or knows a thing she never learned, or her records say she docked at two ports on the same day. The Auditors' findings cannot say whether she is the same person; the law has never been sure it wants to know. It gives the short way: returners find the seam in things, the shortcut through a station, a contract, an argument, and are uncanny company [new].

#### Its Animals

Small and few, because the Fold is mostly a place you pass through [new].

| Animal | One line |
|---|---|
| **Hinges** | Long thin bodies that live straddling a seam, half on each side, so one animal is visible in two far places. A hinge marks a seam; pilots look for them |
| **Knotfish** | Schools that live deep in knots, where there is more room inside than out, and stream out at the mouth when something big comes in |

Nobody has caught a hinge whole. Cutting one at the seam gives you two halves of nothing much, in two places, and an Auditor's case.

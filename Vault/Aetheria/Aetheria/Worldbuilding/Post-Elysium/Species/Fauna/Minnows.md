**"A school that parts for nothing is a warning."**

> Draft, 2026-10-13. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Minnows are hand-sized darts of dark membrane with a row of light organs down each flank. They move in schools of hundreds, and a school in motion flickers like a swarm of sparks, each fish flashing as it turns.

#### What They Eat

Krill. A school grazes the downstream edge of a cloud, thinning it in a ragged band, and follows the filaments of the net from one well to the next.

#### How They Move: the Tell

The school as a whole rides the orbital flow, like krill. Inside it, every fish mills fast, darting and turning in quick jitter that never stops. A school is never still, and never slow.

**What a pilot learns:** a ship can sit at a school's centre and match its flow, but it cannot match the milling. Wearing the school works at range, where the school is one bright smear, and fails up close, where a hunter can see one fish that doesn't jitter. A school also reacts: it parts around hulls and shots. A school that parts around empty space has a ship in the gap.

#### How They Fight

- **Glare.** A startled school fires every light organ at once, a burst that blinds passive sensors for a moment [new]. Pilots use it both ways: spooking a school into a hunter's face covers an escape.
- **Swarming.** A school that is attacked rams back: hundreds of small hits, harmless one by one, that strip paint, sensors and point defences from a ship that lingers [new].
- **Balling.** With a shark close, a school packs into a tight, dim ball. The ball is the warning, not the threat.

#### Harvest

*Netting:* catching a school whole, usually after stunning it with a tuned resonance pulse, the fishing descendant of [[Orbital Forge]]'s Resonant Shatter Swarm resonators [new]. Yields photophores, the light organs, which become flares, decoy charges and sensor glass. Worth more than krill and harder to get: a school that panics glares and scatters.

#### Where They Live

On the downstream edges of krill clouds, and along the filaments between wells. Where krill are, minnows follow; where minnows are, sharks follow.

#### For Designers

- Motion law: orbital flow plus fast milling inside the school; scatter on disturbance.
- Hiding: good at range, bad up close; the milling is the tell.
- Hazard: sensor glare when startled; swarm damage when attacked or lingered in.
- Harvest: photophores, used in flares and decoys; stun-and-net.
- Placement: downstream of krill, along filaments; they draw sharks.

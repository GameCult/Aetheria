**"A school that parts for nothing is a warning."**

> Draft r2, 2026-10-14. Part of [[Spaceborne Fauna]]; niche: schooling forage. Kept from the operator's sketch and r1, with the motion law moved from the orbit to the current. `[new]` marks invention that touches existing canon.

Hand-sized darts of dark membrane with a row of light organs down each flank. A school in motion flickers like a swarm of sparks.

**Eats.** [[Krill]], grazed off the downstream edge of a cloud. Minnows are the middle of the web: almost everything above them eats them.

**Moves: the tell.** The school drifts with the current; inside it, every fish mills fast and never stops. A school parts around hulls and shots.

**What a pilot learns.** A ship can sit at a school's centre and match the current, but not the milling. Wearing the school works at range and fails up close. A school that parts around empty space has something in the gap. A school balled tight and dim has a [[Sharks|shark]] near it.

**On sensors.** One bright smear at range; a storm of tiny returns close in.

**How it hurts.** A startled school fires every light organ at once, blinding passive sensors for a moment; pilots spook schools into a hunter's face to cover an escape. A school that is attacked rams back: hundreds of tiny hits that strip paint, sensors and point defences from a ship that lingers [new].

**Yield.** *Netting*, usually after stunning the school with a tuned resonance pulse. Photophores (the light organs) become flares and sensor glass; meal from the rest.

**Lives.** Downstream of krill, along fronts and skein. Where minnows are, sharks follow.

*Designers:* field; current plus fast milling; scatter on disturbance; hazards are glare and swarm damage.

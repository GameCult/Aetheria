## Megiddo

Defensive technology. Megiddo sells walls, and its brand is holding: a Megiddo barrier does not degrade while the fight lasts.

> A wall is not a weapon. It does not need to be clever, or fast, or new. It needs to be there when the sky opens, and it needs to be there afterwards. Ours have been.

### Doctrine

Megiddo teaches that Elysium is not real. Aether and [[Pseudospace]] are side effects of existing in a simulation anchored only by the cognition of its residents, and the strangeness that has grown since the Timeless’ contingency is evidence of it. To Megiddo, Elysium is an exile, a false land, and waking from it is the return.

They are right about the mechanism ([[Aether]]) and wrong that there is a way out. Nobody goes back.

The doctrine is also their engineering. If reality is held up by attention, then a wall stands because someone refuses to stop believing in it, and Megiddo crews are trained to keep that belief through the moment the sky opens.

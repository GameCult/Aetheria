Stations carry facility gear on a new Facility mount: every recipe names the facility that runs it, every station kind lists the facilities it is fitted with, and every station hull that serves a kind has the Facility row to take them (Zenith and the Arsenal Depot each have one). Nothing runs a recipe yet. Because the hardpoint tints spread across one more type, every hardpoint on the schematic turns a little around the colour wheel.
